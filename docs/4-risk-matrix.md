

# Risk Mitigation & Technical Constraints

## Core Technical Risks

* **Highest Risk Feature:** Job Application

* **The Hazard:** Saving and managing actor profiles and job applications may be difficult. There may also be problems with database connections or missing information.

## The Pivot Strategy

If our primary implementation for this feature fails or takes too long, our backup plan is to:

* Use a simple application form instead of a more complex system.

* Use sample job data instead of a live database during the first version.

* Keep the application process simple and improve it in future updates.
